How Voice Authenticity Analysis Works

🤖 AI Voice Clones: Characterized by unnatural energy stability (RMS variance <0.0009), spectral phase flatness (Wiener entropy), and rigid formant transitions typical of neural TTS vocoders.

📼 Pre-Recorded Robocalls: Show steep frequency dropoffs above 3.8kHz (telephony codec lowpass filtering), synthetic gating between prompt segments, and pre-broadcast compression.

👤 Natural Human Speech: Exhibits dynamic prosodic micro-variations, rich organic harmonics above 4kHz, and natural room acoustic reverberations.
